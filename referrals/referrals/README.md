# Referrals

#### **How do I refer someone to Rebate.fun?**

As a **Classic member**, you can start **referring others and growing your network immediately**.

Your **unique referral code and link are automatically generated** when you join Rebate.fun.\
Simply share your **referral link** to invite others.

> _Custom referral codes are **only assigned by Rebate.fun for official partners and collaborations**._

As a referrer, you'll earn **10% of your referred users' fees**, calculated **after cashback and rebate deductions**.

Referral commissions begin accruing from your **first successful referral**, but will remain **pending** until you reach **Bronze tier**.

Once you reach **Bronze tier**, your accumulated referral rewards become **claimable and withdrawable**.

> **Before reaching Bronze:**
>
> * Rewards will be **credited to your wallet available balance**
> * You may see the **withdraw button enabled**
> * However, **withdrawals are locked and cannot be completed**
> * **Withdrawal is only unlocked after reaching Bronze tier**
> * **Rewards are displayed for transparency, but remain locked until eligibility is met**

Referral rewards accumulate across **all quote assets** and can be claimed once the total exceeds **$1**.

All claimable rewards will be reflected in your **wallet available balance** and can be **withdrawn anytime after unlocking**.

***

### How do I use a referral code?

Simply enter a **referral code on Rebate.fun**, or sign up using a friend's **referral link**.

Using a referral code unlocks **additional benefits** and grants access to **VVIP tier levels**.
