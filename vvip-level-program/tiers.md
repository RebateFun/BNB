# Tiers

The **VVIP Level Program** will officially begin once **Rebate.fun** goes live.

Below is an overview of our **VVIP Levels**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/gitbook (1) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

VVIP members will be eligible for **airdrop rewards following the TGE**.

Your Web3 activities now work for you.

By binding your accounts with **Rebate.fun**, your activity contributes directly to your **VVIP level progression**, helping you advance through higher tiers.

Affiliates earn **10% of referred users’ fees**, calculated after cashback and rebate deductions.

Members who reach **Gold tier and above** enjoy an upgraded referral rate of **25% of platform revenue generated from their direct referrals**.

These earnings are automatically converted into **VVIP progression**, accelerating your advancement through higher tiers.

To unlock enhanced VVIP tiers and exclusive benefits, you may also choose to **contribute your earnings toward your VVIP progression**.

In addition, new users who join **Rebate.fun** through our **partner platforms** may receive designated **VVIP levels** upon onboarding.
