# Exclusive Benefits

#### **Classic — Newcomers**

Your entry point into the Rebate.fun ecosystem.

As a Classic member, you can start referring others immediately.\
\
Referral commissions begin accruing from your first successful referral but remain pending until you reach Bronze tier.

***

#### **Bronze — Connected Members**

Unlock access to the full **Referral Program**.

Upon reaching Bronze tier, your accumulated referral commissions become **claimable**, and all future referral earnings remain withdrawable.

Bronze marks your transition from participation to earning.

***

#### **Silver — Community Access**

Unlock the **Silver Discord Channel**, gaining access to deeper discussions, ecosystem updates, and early opportunities.

***

### **Gold — Inner Community**

Gold marks the beginning of elevated access within the ecosystem.

Platinum members move faster, earn smarter, and gain closer ecosystem access.
